\paragraph{Does diversity track credal width?} \label{sec:width-check} Prediction 1 uses semantic diversity as a proxy for credal width, which we had not checked, and Prediction 4 stratifies by it. Measuring the temperature credal set of Definition~\ref{def:temp-credal} directly from next-token logits on the 500 questions (post hoc), the width of the top token's probability over $\tau \in [0.3, 1.5]$ rises with diversity on all four base models (Spearman $\rho$ from 0.40 to 0.55), and the total-variation diameter of the set is more strongly associated with it ($\rho$ from 0.67 to 0.79). Mean top-token width goes from Q1 to Q4 as follows: 0.41 to 0.69 on Qwen, 0.42 to 0.63 on Mistral, 0.51 to 0.70 on Llama, 0.45 to 0.59 on Gemma. This covers the first token only, which need not be the answer (it is ``The'' on up to 61 of 500 questions). For whole responses the relation can reverse: on the Prediction 4 run, with one instruct model and three samples per temperature, so noisily, the width of the modal answer's frequency falls from 0.85 in Q1 to 0.62 in Q4 (Q1 minus Q4 [0.17, 0.29]). So Prediction 1 tests diversity, which is related to credal width and not the same thing.
